# Corporate legal preparation

Start with the requested artifact, jurisdiction, entity name, entity type and current formation status. Record which facts are supplied and which are independently supported. The names of owners do not establish an equity split, managing member, authority to sign, contributions or profit allocation.

For an operating agreement, identify decisions needed about contributions, ownership, management, voting, distributions, records, transfers, departures, deadlock, dissolution and dispute resolution. Use explicit placeholders for undecided terms. Do not substitute a recommended term for a founder decision. Compare any supplied draft clause by clause and retain a change list.

For formation or recurring compliance, retrieve current primary sources through `research_read`; inspect material passages through `evidence_read`. Distinguish statutory requirements, optional practices and questions needing professional review. Each dated obligation needs its jurisdiction and effective period. Use `boardroom-tax-preparation` through a separate planned task where tax work is substantive.

Write the actual draft or checklist with `artifact_write`, including source references and unresolved decisions. An independent reviewer checks coverage, internal consistency, conflicting source material and missing facts. The output is a reviewable draft; it is not a signed agreement, filing or approval. Do not claim professional review occurred without evidence.

Workflow context: https://github.com/anthropics/claude-for-legal/tree/main/corporate-legal and the installed Boardroom contract/compliance skills. Use only tools actually exposed to this worker.
