# First mission: pricing and sales

Open a fresh chat in your demo project after configuring a model in Harness. Paste this request:

> /boardroom
>
> Use these fictional monthly figures: 20 customers, $100 per customer, $40 variable cost per customer, and $600 fixed costs. We closed 2 of 10 qualified leads. Run a finance and sales mission using only these numbers. Calculate operating profit and break-even customers. Assess the sales conversion rate and ask an independent reviewer to verify both reports. Save the reports for review.

The controller should keep both outcomes in one mission. Expected figures are $600 monthly operating profit, 10 customers to break even, and 20% observed conversion on a small sample. Ask the controller to show the reviewer report and the underlying calculations when the mission is ready.

For a follow-up, say: “Change the monthly fixed cost to $900 and update this mission.” The new operating profit is $300 and break-even rises to 15 customers. The sales inputs stay the same. In a fresh chat in the same project, ask Boardroom to find the mission and show the current results.
